\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Streaming video understanding — a model watches an unbounded stream and answers questions about it later — has produced a dense mechanism literature in two years: KV-cache retrieval~\cite{rekv}, multi-grained compression~\cite{mukv}, query-agnostic pruning~\cite{streammem,infiniPotV,streamingtom}, hierarchical and trained memory~\cite{hermes,weavetime,streamingvlm}, token reduction~\cite{codecsight,eventvstream,statekv,polykv,streamkv}, and agentic memory~\cite{avas,worldmm}. Every paper reports wins. Almost none of them can be compared: judges differ (GPT-3.5 vs GPT-4 class vs proprietary APIs), anchor configurations differ (a CVPR'26 system's published RVS number is produced by a config that is not its repository's default), effective sampling rates are rarely asserted, and clairvoyant ``oracle'' bounds are quoted without null models.

This paper is a measurement study of that landscape, conducted while attempting to beat its strongest representative. We built a controlled stack (Section~\ref{sec:stack}): faithful on-hardware reproductions of MuKV and ReKV, a locally hosted 72B judge whose systematic offset ($\approx -9$ accuracy points vs published GPT-judged numbers) is stable across three independent systems, a standing effective-fps assertion, and a pre-registration discipline in which every gate specifies its pass criteria, its minimum detectable effect, and its multiplicity family before numbers exist.

Against this stack we tested \emph{six mechanisms} drawn from the literature's own admitted openings (Section~\ref{sec:mech}): differential (residual) KV coding, content-adaptive GOP sizing, retrieval-adaptive precision, write-time debiasing, deferred query-time commitment, and a learned commitment policy. \textbf{All six failed under pre-registration}, and each failure carries a root cause: adjacent-frame KV residuals encode rotary position drift, not content change; quantization sensitivity is flat across retrieval frequency; entropy-triggered refinement is inert; per-question policy headroom is a max-over-policies artifact (Section~\ref{sec:null}); and the coverage experiment designed to confirm our own diagnosis returned underpowered-null at $n{=}83$.

Two things survive the carnage. First, a methodological contribution: the \emph{oracle-null protocol} (Section~\ref{sec:null}), which shows that clairvoyant per-question selection bounds — used across the literature to motivate retrieval and allocation mechanisms — are largely artifacts of maximizing over exchangeable policies, and provides the permutation machinery to say so. Second, a diagnosis (Section~\ref{sec:diag}): a ceiling decomposition of every all-wrong question on three benchmarks finds \emph{zero} retrieval failures and a dominant model-capability failure mode concentrated in long-window temporal grounding; the coverage--planner-gain correlation that initially looked causal was \emph{refuted on evidence} by a powered pooled regression — coverage is not the lever, and the field, in our measurement, is optimizing second-order terms (which blocks to select) while the first-order term (whether the model can ground a long temporal window at all) goes unmeasured.

Section~\ref{sec:pareto} gives the honest frontier: after correcting our own frame-rate bug, \sys's multi-grain store matches MuKV's faithful accuracy (paired $p{=}0.203$) and full-fidelity ReKV ($p{=}0.139$) at $8.4$--$11.4\times$ MuKV's published storage in fp16, with the int4-group-scale repair measured separately. We claim no accuracy win — none survives correction — and we show where one might actually live.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A controlled evaluation stack for streaming video-LLM memory: faithful MuKV/ReKV reproductions, a stability-characterized local judge, fps assertions, and a pre-registration template (gates, MDE, multiplicity family) reusable beyond this study.
\item Six mechanism evaluations with root-cause post-mortems, each reproducible from its run directory.
\item The oracle-null protocol: a permutation framework establishing that clairvoyant selection bounds are max-artifacts, with three-benchmark evidence.
\item A coverage--planner-gain correlation and ceiling decomposition identifying long-window temporal grounding as the binding constraint.
\item A corrected Pareto accounting and an explicit map of the design space's dead ends.
\end{enumerate}
