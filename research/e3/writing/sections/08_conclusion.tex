\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We set out to beat the strongest streaming video-LLM memory system and built the instrument that would prove it. The instrument is the result: faithful reproductions that exposed a config-fidelity trap in a CVPR'26 system's own repository, a judge whose stability across three systems makes a literature comparable, a pre-registration discipline that killed six mechanisms — including our own flagship — and an oracle-null protocol that retires a class of motivating bound the field has been quoting uncritically. What survives is a corrected frontier (match, not beat, at an honestly-measured storage cost), a monotone coverage--gain correlation, and a diagnosis that names the real problem: long-window temporal grounding. The next mechanism worth building is the one that attacks that.
