\section{Additional Statistical Detail}
\label{app:extra}

\paragraph{Gate 0 config fidelity.} MuKV argparse-default run: 46.0 (band [47.5, 53.5], FAIL); paper-config run: 50.9 (PASS, expected $\approx$50.5 under the calibrated offset); minimal-pruning sanity: 51.2 (code-path validation). The paper configuration (segment-dominant keep 0.1/0.1/0.8, per-grain topk 20/32/12, $\alpha$ 0.5/0.7/0.8) is now encoded in a versioned script.

\paragraph{W5.4 pooled regression (full).} $n{=}332$ (4 arms $\times$ 83 temporal questions); LPM with cluster-robust standard errors (cluster = question). Specifications: question FE: coefficient $-0.33$ per unit coverage, CI [$-1.99, +1.33$], $p{=}0.69$; arm FE + window length: $+0.56$, CI [$-0.54, +1.66$], $p{=}0.32$; question FE + arm FE: $+0.40$, CI [$-1.33, +2.13$], $p{=}0.65$. Six-bin coverage--accuracy curve non-monotone; the hybrid arm's accuracy gain coincided with \emph{lower} coverage than baseline. Within-question coverage variation (sd 0.019) is small relative to between-question (0.070); the refutation is conditional on the achieved band and store budget, which the text states.

\paragraph{Answer-level int4 (TB6).} Naive per-channel int4 quantization of the store flips 4 of 5 greedy answers at equal prompts; logit-level fidelity (the basis of prior ``4-bit is near-lossless'' claims) does not predict answer-level fidelity. Group-scale int4 is measured at answer level wherever it is reported.

\paragraph{Multiplicity families.} Family A ($m{=}21$): all pairs among the seven full-run RVS arms. Family B ($m{=}15$): the pre-registered comparison subset. Union $m{=}36$. All corrected claims in the text are stated against the family; Holm primary, BH reported.
