\section{The Controlled Evaluation Stack}
\label{sec:stack}

\subsection{Faithful reproductions}
We reproduce two reference systems on one 8$\times$RTX 6000 Ada server (4 GPUs available, CUDA 12.6): ReKV~\cite{rekv} (commit \texttt{1fd9a3d} + Volta-port patches) and MuKV~\cite{mukv} (\texttt{2127b875}). Gate 0 of our study was MuKV's own reproduction: its repository's argparse defaults are \emph{not} its paper's RVS configuration — the published number (57.9 Ego, 7B) requires the segment-dominant keep-ratio setting found only in its shell script. Running the defaults yields 46.0 in our harness; the paper configuration yields 50.9, within 0.5 points of the offset-adjusted expectation (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}). Code path was independently validated by a minimal-pruning run reaching ReKV-level accuracy. \textbf{Lesson institutionalized: runner defaults are not paper configs; every arm encodes its configuration explicitly.}

\subsection{The judge and its stability}
OpenAI APIs are unreachable from our infrastructure; we judge with a locally hosted Qwen2.5-72B (vLLM, TP=4, temperature 0, the RVS grading prompt verbatim from the evaluation codebase). The judge carries a systematic offset versus published GPT-judged numbers — and the offset is the contribution: measured across three independent reproductions it is $-9.2$ (ReKV-0.5B), $-9.6$ (ReKV-7B), and $-8.6$ (MuKV-7B). A stable offset across independent systems is far stronger evidence for \emph{relative} comparability than any single reproduction: within-harness orderings and margins are trustworthy even where absolute numbers are not. Two local judges (72B, 14B) agree at 94\% on a fixed prefix. All letter-based cross-benchmark evaluations use deterministic exact-match scoring, eliminating the judge entirely.

\subsection{Measurement discipline}
Three standing rules, each born from a documented failure: (i) \emph{effective-fps assertion} — every video arm logs and asserts its effective frame rate from frame counts (a double-resampling bug had silently halved our rate against 0.5-fps anchors; Section~\ref{sec:pareto}); (ii) \emph{label-at-emit} — every statistical output carries its test, tail, and complement in the label (a mislabeled tail-complement cost two correction rounds); (iii) \emph{pre-registration} — every experiment declares its primary comparison, multiplicity family, and minimum detectable effect before numbers exist. Multiplicity correction (Holm; BH reported) is applied \emph{symmetrically}: it killed our own headline claim (Section~\ref{sec:pareto}) exactly as it would a reviewer's.

\subsection{Benchmarks}
RVS-Ego (10 Ego4D streams, 1{,}465 questions, GPT-judged open-ended)~\cite{flashvstream,rekv}; StreamingBench real-time split and OVO-Bench real-time mode (both deterministic letter-match; subsets of 180 questions matched in statistical weight, long-horizon videos preferred)~\cite{streamingbench,ovobench}. Ground truth for coverage and temporal analysis comes from each benchmark's annotations.
